Project Mission

About Green Campus Awareness Bot

The Green Campus Awareness Bot (EcoBot) is a modern, student-centric web platform engineered to promote sustainable campus culture, climate awareness, and everyday ecological responsibility among students, teachers, and university staff.

By combining an interactive AI chatbot with gamified challenges, practical environmental tips, and a campus impact dashboard, the project bridges the gap between environmental education and real campus action.

AI-Powered EcoBot

Integrates OpenAI's latest conversational models with a strict environmental system prompt ensuring accurate, student-friendly, and non-hallucinatory guidance. Includes an extensive offline campus knowledge base fallback.

SQLite Data Persistence

Lightweight, zero-configuration local SQLite database storing challenge progress, student activity submissions, categorized tips, and community suggestions securely.

Security-First Architecture

API credentials are kept strictly server-side using environment variables (.env). No secrets or sensitive personal identifiable information (PII) are exposed to client JavaScript.

The 5 Golden Rules of a Green Campus

1. Refuse Before Recycling Say no to disposable straws, promotional handouts, and single-use plastic water bottles.
2. Keep Dry Recyclables Clean Never toss leftover food or liquids into paper and plastic bins; contamination ruins recycling.
3. The Last-Out Switch Off Always switch off overhead fans, lights, and projectors when leaving lecture halls.
4. Every Drop Counts Report dripping faucets to hostel wardens immediately; a single drip wastes 30 liters daily.
5. Pedal & Walk Active Biking and walking across campus cuts carbon emissions to absolute zero while keeping you fit.
6. Plant for the Future Adopt and water a native sapling on campus grounds or nurture a dorm desk plant.
