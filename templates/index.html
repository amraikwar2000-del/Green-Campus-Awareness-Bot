Project Overview

About Green Campus Bot

A comprehensive web application designed to educate college students, faculty, and administrative staff on sustainable campus living.

Higher education institutions produce significant waste, consume high volumes of water, and utilize substantial electrical energy daily. Green Campus Awareness Bot bridges the gap between awareness and concrete action by combining responsive AI guidance with habit-forming gamification.

SDG Alignment

Contributes to UN Sustainable Development Goals 12 (Consumption), 13 (Climate Action), and 15 (Life on Land).

Secure AI Architecture

OpenAI API key protected server-side with local knowledge engine fallback for zero downtime.

Campus Roles & Responsibility
College Students Carry reusable water flasks, avoid canteen disposable plastics, segregate hostel waste, and participate in campus plantation drives.
Faculty & Professors Promote digital assignment submissions, turn off lecture hall projectors after classes, and guide environmental research clubs.
Campus Administration & Staff Maintain washroom plumbing fixtures to stop drips, set AC thermostats to 24°C, and maintain campus compost bins.
